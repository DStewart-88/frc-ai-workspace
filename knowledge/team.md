# Team 88 (TJ²)

- Affiliated with Bridgewater-Raynham Regional High School.
- Doug is a mentor in technical and support roles.

## Standing rules

- Students may not touch the robot while it is enabled, in any mode.

## Practice habits that affect data

- At practice, each battery is run until it's dead before swapping (saves swap and reboot time). Practice logs therefore see far more battery depletion than a typical match.
- Practice matches are usually run in the Driver Station's practice mode. A practice run that goes badly is usually ended early.

## How results are framed for the team

- Charts for the team use current (A), not power (W).
- Team write-ups don't put the brownout problem on battery quality. Many team members worked hard to improve battery quality, and there's little more to gain there.
- Brownout results are stated as matches with a brownout and time remaining at the first one ("eliminates Y brownout matches", "delays the first brownout by X s").
